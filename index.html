HOW TO PLAY

  1. The game is a 3×3 grid of small tic-tac-toe boards.
  2. X starts by playing anywhere on the center board.
  3. The cell you pick decides which small board your opponent plays on next. If you play the top-left cell, they must play on the top-left board.
  4. If the target board is already won or full, the next player can pick any board.
  5. Win a small board by getting three in a row.
  6. Win the game by winning three small boards in a row.
